**Torr Vision Group, University of Oxford** · Dec 2025 – present

Paper: [Data Journalist Agent: Transforming Data into Verifiable Multimodal Stories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11176). ICML 2026 Workshop on Trustworthy AI for Good; ACM CAIAS 2026 Workshop (Spotlight).

**Motivation.** A high-quality news story takes a newsroom team weeks of effort, but can an agent serve as a data journalist end to end?

**Method.** We introduce Data Journalist Agent, a multi-agent framework that turns raw data into multimodal stories in which every claim is grounded in evidence. I built [data2story-pro](https://github.com/QinghongLin/data2story-skill/tree/main/skills/data2story-pro), an optimized version with better multimodal presentation.

**Finding.** The stories outperform expert-written ones on transparency and auditability but fall short on creative design and editorial angle.
