**Torr Vision Group, University of Oxford** · Dec 2025 – present

Paper: _Can VLMs Do Visually Grounded Scientific Reasoning in Counterfactual Worlds?_ (under review)

**Motivation.** Scientific discovery often begins by identifying an anomaly and formulating hypotheses to explain it, but on familiar phenomena VLMs can answer from memory instead of reasoning from the image.

**Method.** I proposed _Visual Deduction_, which modifies one scientific law and edits a real image to show its consequence (e.g. a rainbow with reversed color order). Three multiple-choice questions (Anomaly, Law, Form) diagnose whether a VLM notices the anomaly, attributes it to the right law, and identifies the law's new form.

**Finding.** Across nineteen VLMs, most wrong answers on counterfactual images fall back on the real-world answer, and the main bottleneck is grounding scientific reasoning in visual perception.
