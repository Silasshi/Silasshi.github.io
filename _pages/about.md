I am a third-year MPhys Physics student at the University of Oxford (University College) and an undergraduate researcher in the Torr Vision Group, advised by Dr. Kevin Qinghong Lin.

My research interests are **AI for scientific discovery**, **agentic systems**, and **reinforcement learning for LLMs**. My recent work includes COSMOS, a benchmark that tests whether vision-language models can do visually grounded scientific reasoning in counterfactual worlds; the Data Journalist Agent, a multi-agent system that turns raw data into verifiable multimodal stories; and training LLMs for scientific research planning with rubric-based rewards.

I received a Distinction in my first year, the Nicholas Prize in Physics, and a College Exhibition at University College.
